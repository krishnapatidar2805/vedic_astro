Meet Pandit Gajendra Sharma

Vedic Gajendra Sharma is a respected astrologer, Vastu consultant, and Bhagwat Katha speaker based in Village Kherkheda, near Kaleshwar Mandir on the Sitamau-Suwasra Road, Tehsil Sitamau, District Mandsaur, Madhya Pradesh. With years of dedicated study in Vedic scriptures, Jyotish Shastra, and Vastu Shastra, he has helped thousands of families find peace, prosperity, and clarity through authentic astrological guidance.

His services include Janam Kundli analysis, Kundli Matching, Palmistry, Yagna, Kaal Sarp Dosh Shanti, Rudrabhishek, Vastu Shanti, Pitru Shanti, Navgrah Shanti, Grihshanti, Durga Saptashati Path, and complete Vedic Anushthan — all rooted in traditional wisdom passed down through generations.
